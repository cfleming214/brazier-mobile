What's on screen

  1. System strip. Poller status, detection-to-push p95, checks per minute. Turns red if push delivery fails (AlertPermissionGate).
  2. KPI tiles. Counts by watch state. Each tile filters the list below.
  3. Adapter health. One cell per retailer, showing its rate-governor state. PKC shows backoff after 429s.
  4. Next window. CountdownRing for the nearest confirmed calendar event. Polling switches to burst at T-10.
  5. Live now. WatchCard with time in stock, typical sellout time, comp, a 14-day restock sparkline and a one-tap GO into checkout.
  6. Monitoring. Compact rows with poll tier, retailers and filters. The right column shows state and last-checked time.
  7. Event stream. Raw poll, detect, suppress and push events, so you can see why an alert did or didn't fire.
